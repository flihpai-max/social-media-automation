## Who the user is

A solo operator managing my own social media accounts, using content to drive traffic to my website.

## What they want

Consistent posting across accounts without doing it manually every time, and more website traffic as a result.

## What they're trying to avoid

Inconsistent posting caused by the time cost of manual content creation and scheduling.

## What they've tried before

Manual posting — no scheduling/automation tool in place yet.

## How they'll know it's working

Posts go out consistently without manual effort, and website traffic from social increases.

## What this user is NOT

Not a team or agency managing client accounts — this is a solo operator managing their own.
