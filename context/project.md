## What we're building

A tool that takes user-provided images, generates social media posts from them, schedules the posts, manages multiple social accounts, and repurposes content across platforms.

## Why it matters

Manually creating, formatting, and posting content across multiple social accounts takes hours every week and consistency slips when it's done by hand. This tool automates that work, saving hours and keeping every account posting consistently.

## The shape of "done"

A working tool that takes a provided image, generates a post from it, and automatically publishes it across multiple social media platforms — for one account. No manual approval step; posting is fully automatic.

## What we're NOT building

- No analytics or performance reporting.
- No video support — images only.
- No multi-account management (v1 is one account).
- No content approval/review workflow — posting is fully automatic.
